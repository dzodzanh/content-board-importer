Content Board Importer

A private, internal tool for preparing short-form social media content.

What it does

Content Board Importer is used only by its owner. It reads images from Pinterest boards on the owner's own account, lets the owner edit those images (resize, crop, add text, combine into short videos or photo slideshows), and uploads the results as drafts to the owner's own TikTok accounts. The owner reviews and publishes every draft manually inside the TikTok app.

What it does not do

It does not access other people's Pinterest or TikTok data, does not post to Pinterest, does not publish to TikTok automatically, and is not offered to the public.
