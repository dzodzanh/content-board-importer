Privacy Policy

Content Board Importer · Last updated: October 10, 2026

Content Board Importer ("the App") is a private tool operated by and for a single owner. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Data the App accesses

Pinterest: with the owner's authorization, the App reads the list of boards and the pins (titles, descriptions, links and images) on the owner's own Pinterest account. It requests read-only access and never creates, edits or deletes anything on Pinterest.

TikTok: with the authorization of each account owner, the App reads basic account information (such as display name and account identifier) and uploads media as drafts to that account's TikTok inbox. Drafts are reviewed and published manually by the account owner in the TikTok app.

How data is stored

Access tokens, downloaded images and edited media are stored only on the owner's own computer or private storage. They are used solely to produce the owner's content and are deleted when no longer needed.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Images may be sent to an AI image generation provider only when the owner explicitly chooses to create or modify an image.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected apps settings of Pinterest or TikTok. After revocation the App can no longer access that account.
